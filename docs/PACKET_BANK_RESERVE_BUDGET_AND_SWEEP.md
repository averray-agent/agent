## Why (measured on chain, 2026-11-03)

The reward bank is `AgentAccountCore.positions[signer 0x5a6836…5813][USDC]`
on AAC `0xB1350932…9E57`.

| Date | Liquid | Reserved | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10-11 | 25.545 | — | after signer cleanup |
| 10-23 | 0.545 → 10.705 | 51.65 | Pascal deposited 10.16 |
| 11-03 | 0.705 → 28.705 | 56.65 | Pascal deposited 28 |

- From 10-11 to 10-23: 26.8 USDC was reserved across 43 signer
  `JobCreated` events; only 9.8 USDC came back as `ReservationSettled`.
- The drain comes from reservations, not fraud: money goes into reservations
  faster than it is paid out or released.
- **Today's budget never bites.** The chain job is created at claim time
  (`job-execution-service.js:788` → `gateway.ensureJob`), gated by
  `catalogue-daily-budget.js`. That budget is
  `WORKER_CATALOGUE_GLOBAL_DAILY_BUDGET_RAW=25000000` (25 USDC per rolling
  24 h), about 25× the actual net drain.
- **Reserves stay locked with nobody releasing them.** Nothing in the
  backend calls `finalizeRejectedJob` or `cancelOpenJob`. Rejected jobs
  keep their reserve until someone finalizes them after `DISPUTE_WINDOW`
  (7 d). Jobs reopened by `handleClaimTimeout` sit `Open` with their reserve
  held indefinitely.

## PR 3: reserve sweeper (log mode first)

A scheduled backend job with `RESERVE_SWEEP_MODE=log|enforce` (default
`log`) and `RESERVE_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MIN` (default 60). Per run it does at
most `RESERVE_SWEEP_MAX_TX_PER_RUN` (default 5) transactions, and it stops
if the signer's native DOT gas balance is below the existing gas floor.

It handles these cases, all for signer-posted v3 jobs only:

| State | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Rejected` | `block.timestamp > rejectedAt + DISPUTE_WINDOW` (read the constant from the contract, don't hard-code 7 d) | `finalizeRejectedJob(jobId)` |
| `Claimed` | `block.timestamp > claimExpiry` | `handleClaimTimeout(jobId)`, through the existing gateway method; the job becomes `Open` and is handled by the next row on a later run |
| `Open` | the catalogue listing is `stale` or `closed` **or** the job is older than `RESERVE_SWEEP_OPEN_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (default 14, matching the job's stale date), **and** past `MIN_OPEN_FOR_CANCEL` | `cancelOpenJob(jobId)`, with the signer as poster |

It never acts on `Submitted` (the verifier owns those), on `Disputed`, or
on a job with an active session in the state store (a claim in flight).

- Every action re-reads the job state on chain immediately before sending,
  and skips if it changed.
- Every action records a mutation receipt `(jobId, action)` so a restart
  never sends twice.
- Add the counters `would_finalize_rejected`, `would_timeout_claim` and
  `would_cancel_open`, with their USDC sums, in log mode, and the same
  names without `would_` in enforce. Add them to the PR 2 route and
  `/admin/status`.
- Add a warning `reward_bank_reserve_sweepable` (admin) when more than
  5 USDC is sweepable and the mode is `log`, so the operator knows a switch
  would help.

**Acceptance (each RED when its guard is removed):**
- A Rejected job 1 s inside the dispute window is not swept; 1 s past it
  is.
- An external-poster job is never swept in any state.
- A `Submitted` or `Disputed` job is never swept.
- An Open job with an active session is not cancelled.
- Log mode sends zero transactions (counting signer mock), and enforce
  sends the expected ones.
- The per-run tx cap holds.
- A state change between the read and the send means skip, not revert.
- A restart after a sent tx does not resend (receipt dedupe).
- The gas floor stops the run.
